Our community standards

Keep it kind. Keep it cool.

Everyone helps shape the Corner. These basics help keep it welcoming and fun. Discord's Terms and Community Guidelines also apply.

01

Respect everyone.
No harassment, bullying, hate speech, or personal attacks.

02

Keep content appropriate.
Use the right channels and follow Discord's rules.

03

No spam or disruptive behavior.
Let others enjoy their conversations and games.

04

Protect privacy.
Don't post passwords, private details, or another person's information without permission.

05

Be welcoming.
Help new members feel included, and disagree without being cruel.

06

Ask moderators for help.
If something feels wrong, contact the server's moderation team instead of escalating the situation.
