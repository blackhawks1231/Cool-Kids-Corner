The golden rules

Keep it cool. 😎

Great communities don't happen by accident. Let's make this a place where people feel welcome, respected, and safe to be themselves.

Community information →
01

Respect Everyone.
No harassment, bullying, discrimination, hate speech, threats, or personal attacks. Friendly banter is welcome, but respect others' boundaries.

02

Keep Content Appropriate.
No NSFW, graphic, illegal, or otherwise prohibited content. Keep discussions in their appropriate channels and follow Discord's guidelines.

03

No Spam or Disruption.
Avoid flooding chats, excessive mentions, soundboard abuse, trolling, or intentionally disrupting conversations and voice channels.

04

Protect Everyone's Privacy.
Never share private information, passwords, private messages, or recordings of others without permission. Doxxing is strictly prohibited.

05

Play Fair and Have Fun.
Respect fellow gamers. No intentional griefing, exploiting, cheating, or sabotaging community-organized games and events. Follow the rules of each hosted event.

06

No Unauthorized Advertising.
Do not advertise Discord servers, products, services, referral links, or social media without staff approval. Sharing relevant gaming content in designated channels is welcome.

07

Be Welcoming and Inclusive.
Everyone deserves a chance to participate. Avoid gatekeeping, deliberately excluding members, or mocking others for their experience, skill level, or game preferences.

08

Respect Staff Decisions.
Follow reasonable moderator instructions. If you disagree with a decision, contact server management privately. Respectful appeals are welcome, but public arguments and harassment are not.

09

No Abuse of Authority.
Staff members must follow the same community rules as everyone else. Favoritism, misuse of permissions, retaliation, and unfair punishment are not tolerated.

10

Report Problems, Don't Escalate.
If someone breaks the rules, contact the moderation team or open a support ticket. Avoid retaliating or starting public confrontations.

11

No Evading Moderation.
Using alternate accounts or other methods to bypass timeouts, bans, or restrictions is prohibited.

12

Have Fun and Use Common Sense.
Not every situation can be covered by a written rule.
